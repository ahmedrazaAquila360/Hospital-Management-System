dentition-bp3d.glb — 3D dentition used by the DentalHub dental chart

This file is a modified version of BodyParts3D data:

  BodyParts3D, Copyright© The Database Center for Life Science licensed by
  CC Attribution-Share Alike 2.1 Japan
  https://dbarchive.biosciencedbc.jp/en/bodyparts3d/
  Release 3.0 (2011-09-15), OBJ files with 95% polygon reduction.
  Meshes used: 28 permanent teeth (FMA55680–55706, FMA55798–55799, FMA57140–57143),
  mandible (FMA52748), right and left maxilla (FMA53649, FMA53650),
  gingiva of upper and lower jaw (FMA59763, FMA59764).

Changes made by DentalHub (scripts/build-bodyparts3d-dentition.mts):
  - converted to glTF binary, re-oriented to the chart's axes and recentred;
  - occlusal plane levelled (the source head pose tilts it ~11°);
  - mandible, lower teeth and lower gingiva rotated about the condyles to open
    the mouth for inspection (~20mm between the incisal edges);
  - gingiva extended over the alveolar ridges (the ridge part of each jaw, slightly
    thickened) and its margin moved to each tooth's cemento-enamel junction;
  - maxilla cropped a few millimetres above the root apices;
  - each tooth placed in its own frame, named by FDI number (tooth_11 … tooth_48)
    and split at the cemento-enamel junction into crown and root meshes;
  - meshes simplified (≈119k triangles in total), quantized and Meshopt-compressed;
  - natural enamel, cementum, gingiva and bone shading baked into vertex colours;
  - third molars (18, 28, 38, 48) added from DentalHub's schematic model, because
    BodyParts3D does not include them. They are marked "schematic" in the file.

The still images in public/models/fallback/ (shown on devices without usable 3D
graphics) are renders of this model and are covered by the same licence and credit.

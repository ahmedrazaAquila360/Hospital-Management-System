mouth-realistic.glb — "Realistic mouth" model used by the DentalHub dental chart

This file is a modified version of:

  "Human teeth" by Alexander Antipov (Sketchfab: @Dessen)
  https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/human-teeth-c4c569f0e08948e2a572007a7a5726f2
  Licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)
  https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
  Source file: Sketchfab's GLB download with 2k textures.

Changes made by DentalHub (scripts/build-realistic-mouth.mts):
  - scaled to millimetres and recentred on the chart's axes;
  - occlusal plane levelled;
  - the 32 crowns separated, each placed in its own frame and named by FDI number
    (tooth_11 … tooth_48);
  - the gum, palate and tongue surface split into upper and lower parts, and the lower
    part, with the lower teeth, rotated to open the mouth (~14mm between incisal edges);
  - gum depth below each crown recorded in the tooth nodes (used to size implant posts);
  - materials converted from specular-glossiness to metallic-roughness, with a clear
    coat; textures re-encoded as JPEG; meshes quantized and Meshopt-compressed.

The model has crowns only: no roots, pulp, nerves or jaw bone.
